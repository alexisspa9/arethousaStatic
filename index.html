Welcome to Arethousa

A place made for unhurried island days.

Nestled in the heart of Drios, Arethousa House is an inviting haven for up to eight guests.

The home pairs thoughtful comfort with the easy rhythm of Paros: three welcoming bedrooms, two bathrooms, a fully equipped kitchen and a beautifully decorated veranda. After a day by the sea, gather outdoors for dinner or unwind in the private four-person jacuzzi.
