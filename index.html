Escape to Arethousa House: Your Tranquil Oasis in Dryos, Paros.

+30 693 8899 754

arethousahouse@gmail.com


Nestled in the heart of Dryos, Paros, Arethousa House offers an idyllic retreat just 150 meters away from the pristine shores of Drios Beach. Perfectly positioned within proximity to the azure waters of the Aegean Sea, this charming abode presents an inviting haven for up to 8 guests and 2 infants. Boasting a thoughtfully designed layout, the house features 1 bedroom with a plush double bed, another bedroom with 2 cozy single beds, and a spacious third bedroom offering 1 double bed and a bunk bed with 2 additional single beds – ensuring ample space for families or groups. With 2 bathrooms and convenient amenities like a washing machine, guests can indulge in comfort and convenience throughout their stay.


Immerse yourself in the unique atmosphere of Dryos village, where Arethousa House is ideally situated only 150m away from the beach of Drios and just 1 km from the renowned Golden Beach. Surrounded by a plethora of amenities, including a supermarket, restaurants, cafes, bars, and a bus stop, guests can effortlessly explore the local treasures at their doorstep. After a day of adventure, retreat to the fully equipped kitchen with a dishwasher to whip up delicious meals, or unwind on the beautifully decorated veranda, soaking in panoramic village views and relishing in moments of tranquility. Outdoors, a private jacuzzi for up to 4 guests awaits in the courtyard, offering the perfect setting for relaxation and indulgence. Arethousa House beckons travelers seeking both relaxation and adventure on the enchanting island of Paros, Greece.
